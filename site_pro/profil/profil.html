My Profil

My name is Adrien Naicken. I have played competitive tennis for over 15 years—almost my entire life. This experience has been a great help in shaping me as a person. In fact, I have learned to understand myself and to know how I would react when faced with challenges. Thanks to tennis, I have learned to find solutions quickly. I have learned to control myself and keep my cool when faced with challenges. Throughout this experience, I have learned to work as part of a team during tournaments. Being part of a close-knit team is very important for coaching and for supporting teammates during their matches. Coaching has taught me how to talk to people to re-motivate them when they are slumping. It has taught me to think on my feet in response to events as they unfold and thus react as quickly as possible—ideally with a good solution. Everything I have learned through tennis will help me maintain discipline in my work.

> Adrien Naicken
